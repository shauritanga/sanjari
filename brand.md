# SANJARI BRANDING GUIDE

## 1. Brand Name

**Sanjari**

### Meaning

Sanjari represents people who are aligned, compatible, or moving in the same direction.

The name communicates:

* Compatibility
* Connection
* Shared values
* Meaningful relationships
* Two people whose paths align

---

## 2. Brand Positioning

**Sanjari is a safe and modern dating platform that helps adults meet people who share their intentions, values, and interests.**

The platform should focus on meaningful matches instead of endless swiping.

### Brand promise

**Helping people find someone who matches their path.**

## 5. Brand Personality

Sanjari should feel:

* Warm
* Trustworthy
* Mature
* Modern
* Respectful
* Safe
* Inclusive
* African-inspired
* Simple
* Relationship-focused

The brand should not feel childish, overly sexual, desperate, or focused only on casual encounters.

## 6. Brand Colours

### Primary colour

**Sanjari Coral**

Hex: `#E85D75`

Use it for:

* Primary buttons
* Important icons
* Match celebrations
* Active navigation items
* Calls to action

This colour feels warm, emotional, energetic, and welcoming.

### Secondary colour

**Deep Plum**

Hex: `#4A2545`

Use it for:

* Headings
* Navigation
* Premium sections
* Dark backgrounds
* Brand identity elements

It gives the platform a mature and trustworthy appearance.

### Accent colour

**Soft Gold**

Hex: `#F4B860`

Use it for:

* Premium features
* Verified profiles
* Highlights
* Special matches
* Subscription badges

### Background colour

**Warm White**

Hex: `#FFF9F7`

Use it as the main light-mode background.

### Surface colour

**Soft Rose**

Hex: `#FCEAEC`

Use it for:

* Cards
* Selected interests
* Notifications
* Empty-state illustrations

### Text colour

**Charcoal**

Hex: `#252126`

Use it for normal text and headings.

### Secondary text

Hex: `#746B72`

Use it for descriptions, dates, labels, and supporting text.

### Success colour

Hex: `#2E9D73`

Use it for:

* Successful verification
* Completed profile steps
* Confirmations
* Online or active status

### Error colour

Hex: `#D64550`

Use it for:

* Validation errors

* Safety warnings

## 9. Logo Concept

The logo should be simple enough to work as an app icon.

### Recommended concept

Create two curved shapes moving toward each other to form:

* The letter **S**
* Two people
* Two connected paths
* A subtle heart shape in the centre

The symbol should communicate alignment without using an obvious heart icon.

### Logo appearance

* Rounded edges
* Minimal details
* Balanced symmetry
* Modern flat design
* Easy to recognise at small sizes
* Suitable for both light and dark backgrounds

### Logo colours

Primary version:

* Sanjari Coral symbol
* Deep Plum wordmark

Dark background version:

* Warm White symbol and wordmark

Monochrome version:

* All white
* All black
* All Deep Plum

---

## 10. App Icon

The app icon should contain only the Sanjari symbol.

Recommended design:

* Deep Plum background
* Coral connected-path symbol
* Rounded square
* No text
* No detailed illustration
* No photograph
* No obvious location pin

The icon should remain recognisable when displayed at a small size.

---

## 11. Wordmark

Write the name as:

**Sanjari**

Use sentence case, not all capital letters.

Preferred:

**Sanjari**

Avoid:

**SANJARI**

The letter shapes should be rounded and friendly.

The dot above the letter “j” can use the coral accent or become part of the connecting-path symbol.

---

## 12. Brand Voice

Sanjari should communicate like a helpful and respectful platform.

### Tone

* Friendly
* Calm
* Positive
* Clear
* Encouraging
* Respectful
* Safety-conscious

### Words to use

* Connect
* Match
* Compatible
* Meaningful
* Respect
* Safe
* Discover
* Intentions
* Shared interests
* Your path

### Words to avoid

* Hot singles
* Hook up now
* Find love instantly
* Perfect soulmate guaranteed
* Desperate
* Sexy matches nearby
* Someone is waiting for you

Avoid language that pressures users or makes unrealistic promises.
