# Safety Escalation Runbook

This runbook applies to Sanjari staff with the `reports.resolve` permission. It does not replace local emergency services or legal advice.

## Immediate Danger

1. Preserve only the minimum report evidence needed for review.
2. Escalate the case to a senior moderator and record the reason and target.
3. Use a human-reviewed `suspend` action when continued access creates immediate platform risk.
4. Tell the reporter to contact local emergency services and a trusted person. Do not promise a police response or personal intervention.
5. Record the escalation, actor, timestamp, and follow-up owner in the case audit trail.

## Suspected Underage User

1. Escalate immediately to the designated age-safety reviewer.
2. Restrict discovery and messaging through the approved moderation action.
3. Do not request extra identity documents in chat or expose the reporter's identity.
4. Follow the approved legal and law-enforcement disclosure process before sharing information.

## Scam, Impersonation, Or Payment Requests

1. Review the report, evidence references, risk signals, and recent account activity.
2. Remove or restrict the offending content where authorized.
3. Suspend or escalate when the evidence supports ongoing user harm; automated risk score alone is insufficient for a permanent ban.
4. Notify the reporter with safety guidance without disclosing confidential investigation details.

## Abuse Spike Or Provider Incident

1. Open an incident and identify the affected queue, provider, and time window.
2. Disable the affected provider or feature using its configured kill switch.
3. Preserve audit and queue records, redact sensitive content from incident notes, and assign an incident owner.
4. Re-enable only after a verification test and documented approval.

## Required Closure Checks

- Case status, action reason, actor, target, and timestamp are present.
- Appeals remain available where policy allows.
- Evidence retention and deletion deadlines are recorded.
- Legal/support notification requirements are satisfied.
