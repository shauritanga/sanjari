# Privacy And Legal Review Checklist

Requires qualified legal review before launch:

- Terms of service.
- Privacy policy.
- Community guidelines.
- Data retention schedule.
- Age assurance workflow.
- Biometric and identity-document verification.
- Subscription cancellation, renewal, refund, and grace-period language.
- Law-enforcement and emergency disclosure process.
- Data export and deletion process.

The implementation includes localized Safety Centre guidance, audited export/deletion requests, expiring local export artifacts, evidence retention purges, and the safety escalation runbook. Legal approval is still required for the final wording, retention schedule, emergency disclosure process, and launch decision.
